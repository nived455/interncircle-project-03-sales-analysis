## One-Page Business Brief

### Context
Management requires an analytical breakdown of 2025 e-commerce sales (`2,000` orders, `$2,349,957` total revenue) to answer three core operational questions:
1. **Seasonality:** Which months and quarters generate the highest revenue, and what is our month-over-month (MoM) growth trajectory?
2. **Category Performance:** Which product categories drive the most total revenue, order volume, and Average Order Value (AOV)?
3. **Customer Concentration (Pareto Principle):** What share of total revenue is generated by our top 10% and top 20% of customers, and what targeted actions should we take next?

### Key findings
* **Monthly Revenue & Seasonality:** Total 2025 revenue reached **$2,349,957** (monthly average: **$195,830**; overall AOV: **$1,174.98**). Revenue peaked in **August 2025 at $223,773 (+11.1% MoM)**, making **Q3** the highest-performing quarter (**$614,159**). The sharpest downturn occurred in **June 2025 at $175,409 (-16.1% MoM)**.
* **Category Performance:** **Fashion** (**$843,294** | **35.9%** of revenue | 708 orders) and **Electronics** (**$702,523** | **29.9%** of revenue | 602 orders) together account for **65.8% of total revenue**. **Home** recorded the highest Average Order Value at **$1,231.02** ($462,864 across 376 orders), while **Books** trailed with **$341,276** (14.5% of revenue; 314 orders; $1,086.86 AOV).
* **Customer Concentration (Pareto Analysis):** Out of `299` active customers, the **top 10% of customers (29 buyers) generate 18.2% of total revenue ($428,393)**, spending an average of **$14,772 per customer** (1.88x the overall average of $7,859). Expanding to the **top 20% of customers (59 buyers) captures 32.5% of total revenue ($764,458)**.

### Recommendations
1. **Launch a VIP Retention Tier for the Top 10% Cohort:** Enrol the top 29 customers driving **18.2% ($428.4K)** of revenue (and top 59 customers driving **32.5% / $764.5K**) into a VIP loyalty program offering free express shipping and 48-hour early access to `Fashion` and `Electronics` releases. Retaining 90% of the top 20% cohort protects **$688K+** in annual revenue.
2. **Deploy a Mid-Year Promotional Campaign to Offset the June Slump:** Counter the **-16.1% MoM ($33,670)** revenue drop in June by running a targeted mid-year flash sale on `Electronics` and `Fashion` during the first week of June, aiming to recover **$20K–$25K** toward the **$195.8K** monthly average.
3. **Cross-Sell High-AOV `Home` Items at Checkout:** Because `Fashion` and `Electronics` drive **65.5% of all order traffic (1,310 orders)** while `Home` commands the highest AOV (**$1,231.02**), introducing a 10% checkout bundle discount on `Home` products for `Fashion`/`Electronics` buyers can lift overall store AOV from **$1,175 toward $1,220+**.

### Next steps
* **Sprint 1 (Weeks 1–2):** Export the top 29 customer IDs to the CRM system and run a 60-day A/B test comparing VIP loyalty perks against a standard discount code.
* **Sprint 2 (Weeks 3–4):** Integrate product return rates, customer acquisition cost (CAC), and category gross margins into the dataset to analyze net profitability alongside gross revenue.
